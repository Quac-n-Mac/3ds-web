    🤖 AI Player

    NVIDIA's NitroGen plays for you (System 1: it reacts to the top screen several times a second), and a planner can steer it (System 2: a vision-language model that reads both screens and your notes every few seconds). Your own keys and controller still work.
    System 2 · Planner
    Looking ahead pauses the game to try a few futures with NitroGen (run on, jump, dash and jump, unsteered) and plays the best one. Near dangers, NitroGen plays on its own, saving the game now and then; when it fails it goes back to a save from before and looks ahead there, and it remembers the place and the moving thing beside it, so it takes care there, and near things that look like it, the next time.
    It steers NitroGen: where to head and which buttons to favour or avoid; in menus, dialogues and on the bottom screen it presses and touches by itself. Planner models come from huggingface.co (onnx-community, pinned revisions; Qwen3.5: Apache 2.0, LFM2.5-VL: LFM Open License v1.0) and run with Transformers.js from cdn.jsdelivr.net. Download them below.
